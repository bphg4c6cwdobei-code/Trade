House rules

  • Trend-aligned trades trigger on the 1H close. Counter-trend longs need the 4H to agree first.
  • Never switch timeframe mid-trade to avoid a stop.
  • Price between 1.505 and 1.528 is a no-trade zone. Chop is not a setup.
